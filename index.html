About Me

I am Juliet Ibrahim, an Urban Planner, Data Analyst, and Climate Resilience Advocate. I combine my background in urban planning and data analysis with a passion for empowering communities through education, innovation, and climate action.

I am motivated by a vision of resilient cities where knowledge and technology help people thrive in the face of environmental and social challenges.

Perception and Attitude of Women Towards Family Planning in Narayi Community

Conducted a research project assessing awareness, perceptions, and challenges women face in accessing family planning services in Narayi Community. Using Kobo Tool, Power BI, Power Query, and PowerPoint, the study analyzed survey data to uncover key insights:

  • 87.04% of women were aware of family planning methods, while 12.96% were not.
  • Implants, natural methods, condoms, and pills were the most commonly known methods.
  • Internet (100%), family/friends (99%), and healthcare providers (64%) were major sources of information.
  • Challenges included partner opposition (27%), distance (24%), cost and cultural/religious beliefs (19%), and lack of information (10%).
  • Perception: 44% said family planning is very accessible, while 3% said not at all accessible.

The project concluded with recommendations to increase awareness, address cultural barriers, and improve healthcare access for women in Narayi Community.

Publications

Flood Control in Kaduna South L.G.A

Published in IIARD International Journal of Geography & Environmental Management (Vol. 9, No. 5, 2023). This study applied Ecohydrological principles to address annual flooding in Barnawa, Down Quarters, and Chenchengi Layout. Using GIS tools, the research identified affected structures and farmland, and recommended relocation of developments along the River Kaduna bank, alongside strict enforcement of flood management policies.

Corresponding Author: julietoguche@gmail.com, adamaizuchukwu@gmail.com
